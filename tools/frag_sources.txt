\subsection{Where the numbers come from}
The three design factors and the three performance indicators are taken
from the statement and from the attached data set; no value in this report
is imported from an external source. The factor ranges that bound the
feasible set are the ranges actually observed in the data, so every point
in the optimisation is a design that has been measured rather than
interpolated, and the prediction intervals reported later widen exactly
where that guarantee stops. The three model forms were selected by
cross-validated error on the attached data, so the coefficients are
estimates from this sample and not constants carried over from the
literature; the only quantities imported from outside are the flow
regime and the correlation structure, and both are stated with their
conditions rather than applied silently.
